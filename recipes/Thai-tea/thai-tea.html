Thai Tea

An iconic Thai drink that can be bought at almost every soi

This simple and sweet(very sweet!) drink is a factor that prevents all Thais from going insane with the heat

Ingredients

  • Dried Thai tea leaves
  • Sweet condensed milk
  • Evaporated milk
  • Sugar
  • Hot water
  • Ice

No quantity is shown because it's up to one's preference. As long as you mix all of these ingredients and the tea becomes bright orange, you do it correctly.

Cooking

  1. Brew Thai tea leaves with hot water and filter the leaves out
  2. Mix it with sweet condensed milk and sugar until satisfied
  3. Add evaporated milk until it's creamy and bright orange
  4. Pour it on a cup of ice
