My Phanang Curry

Phanang curry is my most favorite Thai dish. It is very simple to make, yet complex in its fragrance and flavor. Its color may look intimidating, but the heat is only warm. Salty, creamy, and spicy at the right amount.

Once one understands how to cook it, they can grasp the idea on how to cook most Middle region Thai curries.

Ingredients

  • Any meat 500 grams
  • Phanang curry paste 2 tablespoons
  • Coconut milk 500 milliliters
  • Palm sugar 2 tablespoons
  • Fish sauce 3 tablespoons
  • Kaffir lime leaves 2 grams
  • Red chili peppers 20 grams
  • Water 200 milliliters

Please note that you don't have the right amount or add them all. Thai cooking has no fixed methods and can be adjusted to one liking. Add little by little until you get the perfect flavor for yourself.

Cooking

  1. Add half of the coconut milk and stir on medium heat until its oil is visible
  2. Add the curry paste and fry it with that oil
  3. Once you see red oil comes out, add all the meat and leave it until the outer layer is cooked
  4. Add almost all of the coconut milk and wait until it's heated up again

    5. We don't add it all right away because in case it's already creamy enough or too creamy, we can choose to add or not add it later

  5. Then add water to dilute it so we have more amount of the soup
  6. Taste test to see whether it should be saltier or sweeter and adjust to your liking
  7. Once the taste is perfect, turn off the heat and add chili peppers and kaffir lime leaves. Then stir.

    8. Tip: we can slice kaffir lime leaves into thin slices before hand and garnish the curry on top at the end for a better presentation
